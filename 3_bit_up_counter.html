Educational Explanation

Press Start or Single Step to begin. The first flip-flop toggles from the external clock, while the following stages receive their clock from the preceding Q output.
For an asynchronous ripple counter, propagation delay is real: state changes ripple from Q0 to Q1 to Q2 rather than occurring simultaneously.
